What the spread in market estimates tells you

Two public summaries put the 2024 AI governance market at $0.89 billion and $226.3 million — a fourfold gap. That is not an error to average away. It shows the category has no settled boundary: one vendor counts compliance platforms, another counts a narrower governance-software slice. Treat any single market-size headline as a claim about a definition, and read the definition before the number. The direction, though, is consistent across both: governance spending grows several times faster than the broader software market, while incident counts climb alongside deployment.

The IBM findings cut the other way against complacency. Breach costs fell globally in 2025, helped by faster detection, yet the U.S. average rose to a record and nearly every organization hit by an AI-related incident was missing basic access controls. The audit question is therefore concrete, not philosophical: who can reach the model, what did it do, and can anyone prove it afterward?
